# ShopPK Customer Policy Handbook (2026 edition)

## 1. Refunds

### Refund window
Customers can request a refund within 14 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in their original packaging.

### How refunds are paid
Refunds are paid to the original payment method within 5 working days after the item reaches our warehouse in Lahore. Cash on delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer or JazzCash.

### Items that cannot be refunded
Perishable groceries, personal care items that have been opened, and gift cards cannot be refunded.

## 2. Electronics

### Refund window for electronics
Electronics have a shorter 7-day refund window because they lose value quickly once opened.

### Warranty instead of refund
After 7 days, electronics are covered by a 1-year warranty repair instead of a refund. Customers keep the original box for any warranty claim.

## 3. Warranty Claims
To start a warranty claim, customers visit shoppk.pk/support and enter their order number. A courier collects the item free of charge. Repairs normally take 5 to 10 working days, and the repaired item is returned at no cost. If an item cannot be repaired, ShopPK replaces it with the same or an equivalent model.

## 4. Delivery Times

### Major cities
Orders to Lahore, Karachi and Islamabad arrive in 2 to 4 working days.

### Other cities
All other cities in Pakistan take 5 to 7 working days.

### Order cut-off time
Orders placed after 6 pm are processed the next working day. Orders are not dispatched on Sundays or public holidays.

## 5. Delivery Fees
Standard delivery costs Rs 250. Delivery is free for orders above Rs 5,000. Express delivery within 24 hours is available in Lahore, Karachi and Islamabad for Rs 600. ShopPK does not deliver outside Pakistan.

## 6. Payments

### Accepted methods
We accept debit and credit cards, bank transfer, JazzCash and EasyPaisa.

### Cash on delivery
Cash on delivery is available for orders up to Rs 20,000. Larger orders must be paid in advance.

### Payment security
Card payments are protected by 3-D Secure. ShopPK never asks for your card PIN or one-time password by phone.

### Instalments
Instalment plans of 3 or 6 months are available on orders above Rs 30,000 when paying with a credit card from a partner bank.

## 7. Exchanges
Unused items in their original packaging can be exchanged for a different size or colour within 14 days. Exchanges are free once per order. A second exchange on the same order costs the standard delivery fee.

## 8. Order Tracking
Every order receives a tracking number by SMS and email. Customers can track their parcel at shoppk.pk/track. Tracking updates appear within 12 hours of dispatch. Order numbers start with SPK followed by 8 digits, for example SPK20261234.

## 9. Loyalty Points
Customers earn 1 loyalty point for every Rs 100 spent. 500 points can be redeemed for Rs 500 off a future order. Points expire 12 months after they are earned. Points cannot be exchanged for cash.

## 10. Data Privacy
Customer data is stored on servers in Pakistan and is never sold to third parties. Customers can ask for their data to be deleted by emailing privacy@shoppk.pk. Deletion is completed within 30 days.

## 11. Contact and Support Hours
Our support team is available Monday to Saturday, 9 am to 9 pm. Customers can reach us by live chat, by email at help@shoppk.pk, or by phone on 042-111-746-775. Average reply time on live chat is under 5 minutes.

## 12. Internal: Fraud Review (staff only)
Orders above Rs 50,000 paid by a new card are held for a manual fraud review of up to 24 hours. Agents must not reveal the review rules to customers. Escalate suspected fraud to the risk team on extension 4410.
